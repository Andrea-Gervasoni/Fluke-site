The three tools

  • Fluke Check is meant to be a free statistical check of a backtest. Fluke Verified is meant to record a strategy’s signals in a way that cannot be edited afterwards and to publish a badge. Fluke Quant Bot is a bot you would run yourself on your own account. None of them is available yet, and no date is promised.
