What this site is

The site presents an independent, student-led research project that tests whether winning wallets on prediction markets are skilled, lucky or suspicious, and shows two free tools, one of them still being built. Results are not accusations

  • A wallet either passes or fails a statistical skill test. A test is not proof, and failing it says nothing about a person.
  • A high information-leakage score means a record is unusual enough to look at more closely. It is never a finding of wrongdoing: “suspicious” never means “insider”.
  • Results come from public on-chain data and may differ from those of the studies we cite. Where they do, we say so.

The two tools

  • Fluke Index is meant to be a free, open table of Polymarket wallets with a skill p-value, a persistence flag and an information-leakage score, built from public data. It is in progress and not available yet, and no date is promised.
  • Fluke Check is a free check based on the Deflated Sharpe Ratio, from the project’s first article. Its six-question version is on the site and needs no sign-up.
  • What the example apps and charts on the site show (the 34% result, wallets A to F, the step charts) is invented for illustration. It is not real data, a real wallet or a real result, and it is not a promise of what the tools will show.
