The short version. No cookies, no ad trackers, no accounts, no payments. We count visits without identifying anyone. The only personal data we keep is an email address, and only if you write to us to be told when the Fluke Index opens. You can have it removed any time.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Andrea Celeste Gervasoni, an individual acting on their own behalf (not yet a company), referred to here as “Fluke”, “we” or “us”.

For anything about your data, write to privacy@usefluke.it. We have not appointed a data protection officer, because the law does not require one for a project of this size.

Cookies and your browser

This site sets no cookies and uses no advertising or tracking technologies.

The only thing the site writes to your browser is one entry in session storage, named fluke-seen, so that the opening animation is shorter if you come back during the same visit. It stays on your device, is never sent to us and disappears when you close the tab. Because it only provides a feature you asked for, it needs no consent.

Fonts, scripts and images are served from the site itself: nothing is loaded from third-party font or tracking services. The only third-party script is the Cloudflare Web Analytics counter described above. The site links to outside sources (studies, articles, a dataset); they only see you if you follow a link.

Getting notified

There is no sign-up form, no account and no mailing list. The “Get notified” button opens your own email app with a message to hello@usefluke.it. If you send it, we keep your address and the date, and use them once: to tell you that the Fluke Index is open, or that the plan has changed. Then we delete them.

It is not a newsletter, we do not add you to any other list, and we do not pass the address on. You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to privacy@usefluke.it.

The research data

The research reads public, on-chain records of Polymarket trades. A wallet address is a string of characters, not a name: we do not try to find out who is behind a wallet, and we do not combine the records with any other data about people. The site shows no real wallets yet; the table on the computer screen is illustrative.

Who else sees your data

We use a few providers who process data on our instructions: our hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc.)), which also runs the visit counter, our domain name service and the forwarding of email sent to our addresses (Cloudflare, Inc., United States, certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework), and our email provider. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising. We may share data if a law or a public authority requires it.

Where a provider is outside the European Economic Area, we rely on an adequacy decision (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, a portable copy, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Where processing relies on consent you can withdraw it whenever you like, without affecting what happened before.

Write to privacy@usefluke.it. We answer within one month. If you think we have handled your data wrongly you can complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority of the country where you live.

Also worth knowing

  • There are no accounts and no payments on this site, and Fluke never asks for exchange keys or wallet access.
  • We make no decisions about you by automated means and we do not profile you.
  • The site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
  • We keep the site served over HTTPS and limit access to the few accounts that need it. No system is perfectly secure; if there is a breach that puts you at risk we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.
  • If we change this policy we change the date at the top and, if the change matters, we tell the people who asked to be notified.
